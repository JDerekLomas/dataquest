Savvas Data Show Data literacy · Grades 9–11

A series of interactive data lessons

Learn to read the world, one dataset at a time.

Every lesson hands students a real, public dataset and a single starting point, then guides them to discover the pattern themselves — the way a working data scientist does. No equations to memorize; just a question worth answering, a graph that earns each reveal, and a finding the student names in their own words. Four lessons, four core literacies.

9 steps each · ~15 min Narrated Mastery-gated & self-paced 100% real cited data

Spatial patternsLesson 1

Mapping the Ring of Fire

Start from one earthquake under Kobe and watch 800 more trace a fiery arc around the Pacific. Maps, geometry, and a histogram become evidence.

The Broad Street Pump

Scatter & zonesLesson 3

Are We Alone?

Time-seriesLesson 4

The Breathing Planet

Follow 68 years of CO₂ on a Hawaiian volcano. Separate the trend from the seasonal "breathing," and watch a straight line fail to keep up.

Data: NOAA Mauna Loa CO₂ (the Keeling Curve)
Start lesson →review mode
Every dot is real. No lesson uses invented or "sample" values — each pulls from a named, public dataset (USGS, HistData/John Snow, NASA, NOAA), and every count and statistic on screen is computed from that data. Where a field was never measured, the lesson says so rather than filling it in.

Savvas Data Show · classroom prototype · a four-lesson data-literacy series. Each lesson is a self-contained, file-openable scrollytelling experience with narration, a notice-and-wonder explore step, mastery gating, and a reviewer-feedback mode (?review=true).
